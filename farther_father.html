Farther, Father

Fragile Father, &nsp;&nsp;&nsp; man made of clay. Nightmare monster, &nsp;&nsp;&nsp; stare of dismay. Brooding boulder, &nsp;&nsp;&nsp; shaman of my soul. Rock turning older, &nsp;&nsp;&nsp; my heart grows colder. Tender Father, &nsp;&nsp;&nsp; "Your eyes blaze with light!" Farther, Father, &nsp;&nsp;&nsp; "This darkness, too bright."

-- Eric Nelson
