Demon Day

I walked one day when it was mild.
I sold my sight and killed my child.
I walked until the sun went down.
I went to sleep and found the clown.

I woke to Death and saw it straight
but Death was wise and set new bait.
It asked me if I loved my wife.
I said I dearly loved my life.

Death came in stealth and robbed my days
With no pity, or smile, or play.
And I went down to Hell in sleep.
And there I learned to weep.


—Eric Nelson
