Pastor Prayer

Lord, help me to be the Jesus I need to be today.
If meek and mild, give me courage.
If difficult and demanding, give me grace.
If fierce and fearsome, give me humility.

But whichever Jesus I need to be today,
Let me live him out of love,
And act him for the good of your people.
Amen.

-- Eric Nelson
