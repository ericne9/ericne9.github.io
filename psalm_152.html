Psalm 152

Lord, guide me-- for I'm wandering through my life. I'm like a hiker without a compass, circling in a dark woods, afraid. Show me the way through the forest of my heart. Point out the road abandoned long ago or take me beside a river that flows to the sea. I don't ask for the destination, or even a map. Your finger pointing, is direction enough for me. Your hand on my back, the courage I need.

-- Eric Nelson
