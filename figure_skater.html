Figure Skater

She holds her orbit with her arms
embracing the air as she revolves.
Balanced on the blade edge
her shadow moves as she moves
more faithful than body is to mind.

The ice records what I cannot--
thrusts, axles, turns and jumps
how she draws the world into a spiral
or with a gesture rises into a whirlwind
and with a bow unwinds and brushes
the outer edge of perfection.


-- Eric Nelson
