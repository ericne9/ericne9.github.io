Love is a Circle

Who listens to the listeners?
No one, but you or I.
Who gives to the givers?
No one, but you or I.
Who loves the lovers?
No one, but you or I.

Love is not a line.
It is not an arrow shot from heart to heart.
Love is a circle
turning around a radiant center.
Be willing to be dizzy.
Step in and join the dance.


—Eric Nelson
